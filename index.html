Stat Priority & Combat Mechanics

🔮 Warlock Stat Priority

1. Shadow Spell Damage / Spell Power: The supreme leveling stat. Directly increases damage and healing of Corruption, Drain Life, Siphon Life, and Agony!
2. Stamina: Vital health pool. In Classic, Stamina = Mana because Life Tap converts health directly into spell resources!
3. Intellect: Expands starting mana pool and increases spell critical strike chance.
4. Spirit: Improves health and mana regeneration outside of combat.

🗡️ Rogue Stat Priority & Weapon Speeds

1. Agility: 1 Agility = 1 Attack Power, +Armor, +Dodge, and 29 Agility = 1% Critical Strike chance.
2. Strength: 1 Strength = 1 Attack Power. Directly scales white melee and ability strikes.
3. Weapon Speed Golden Rule:
• Main Hand: SLOW (2.6 – 2.8 speed): Sinister Strike damage is calculated from top-end weapon damage, so slow weapons hit significantly harder!
• Off-Hand: FAST (1.3 – 1.6 speed): Maximizes auto-attack swing frequency for continuous Instant Poison applications and Combat talent procs!
