Critical Class Quest

📍 Urs'endris Exact Location & Cave Pathing Clarification:

Many players get confused by the coordinates on Zephras Isle! Urs'endris is NOT in the cave. Urs'endris is the quest giver standing outside at the East Pylon Watchtower (69.8, 61.7).
The coordinates (53.7, 65.0) are for the cave where Ur'endra lives, which you must approach strictly from the northern side.

1
Hit Level 10 & Talk to Trainer: In the town hub / High Order Enclave on Zephras Isle, speak with Druid Trainer Lotheluum Starbreeze. Pick up the opening prerequisite quest: "The Great Ursera Spirit".
2
Find Urs'endris at East Pylon Watchtower (Coords: 69.8, 61.7):
Head eastward toward the coast, northeast of Valanaar and near the waterfall below the Shrine of Akir. Speak to Urs'endris at coordinates (69.8, 61.7). Turn in the prerequisite quest and accept "Strength and Mercy". Urs'endris asks you to put down his mad mate, Ur'endra.
3
Locate the Overlook Standing Stones Cave (Coords: 53.7, 65.0):
Travel west into the bluffs between the Shen'dar Highlands and the Gustberry Lowlands toward coordinates (53.7, 65.0).
4
⚠️ CRITICAL PATHING WARNING — Approach from the NORTH:
The southern face of the bluff has sheer vertical rock walls that cannot be climbed. Circle around to the NORTHERN side of the mountain ridge to find the open dirt ramp leading up into the cave entrance!
5
Defeat Ur'endra:
Enter the northern cave entrance. Pull Ur'endra with Wrath from max range, apply Moonfire, keep Rejuvenation on yourself, and finish her with melee swings.
6
Turn In & Unlock Bear Form!
Return to Urs'endris at the East Pylon Watchtower (69.8, 61.7). Turn in "Strength and Mercy" to instantly unlock Bear Form, plus Maul (Rank 1), Growl (Rank 1), and Demoralizing Roar (Rank 1)!
Once complete, take the High Order portal to Stormwind City to start mainland questing!

The Golden Rule of Feral Weapons (Critical Knowledge!)

⚠️ Weapons are STAT STICKS in Classic / Vanilla / WoW Forever

When you shift into Cat Form or Bear Form, your weapon's DPS, swing speed, and weapon damage range do NOT affect your paw auto-attacks!
• Cat Form attacks at a fixed 1.0-second weapon speed.
• Bear Form attacks at a fixed 2.5-second weapon speed.
• What actually determines your damage? The stats on the weapon (+Strength, +Agility, +Attack Power) and Feral Attack Power! A slow 50 DPS mace with zero stats is completely useless compared to a fast green staff with +15 Agility!

Feral Stat Hierarchy

1. Agility (Agi) — Supreme for Cat

• 1 Agility = 1 Attack Power (AP) in Cat Form.
• 20 Agility = 1% Critical Strike chance.
• Also provides valuable Dodge chance and raw Armor for Bear tanking.

2. Strength (Str) — Supreme for Bear & Cat

• 1 Strength = 2 Attack Power (AP) in both Bear and Cat forms!
• Multiplied further by the Heart of the Wild talent (+20% Strength in Cat form).

3. Stamina (Stam) — Health Pool

• 1 Stamina = 10 Hit Points.
• In Bear Form, multiplied by the +20% Stamina bonus from Heart of the Wild. Essential for surviving dungeon boss cleaves.

4. Spirit (Spi) — Regeneration

• Restores Health and Mana out of combat.
• Synergizes directly with your Alliance Skyborne racial Read Ley Line to instantly recover mana between shifts without drinking.

Complete Feral Weapon & Off-Hand Arsenal Matrix

Druids have unique weapon restrictions. Knowing what you can wield, what weapon trainers teach them, and why shields are prohibited is vital for your journey.

Two-Handed Staves (2H) ✓ Baseline Allowed
Best leveling category! Druids learn Staves automatically at Level 1. Staves carry enormous stat bundles (+Strength, +Agility, +Stamina) and late-game armor (e.g. Warden Staff +260 Armor).
Two-Handed Maces (2H) ✓ Trainable (Ironforge)
The heavy hitter! Must be trained by Weapon Master Bixi Wobblestaff in the Military Ward of Ironforge (cost: 10 silver). Unlocks Smite's Mighty Hammer (+11 Str, +4 Agi) and Manual Crowd Pumper.
One-Handed Maces (1H) ✓ Baseline Allowed
Allowed by default. Can be paired with an Off-Hand item ("Held in Off-hand"). Good if you find a very high Agility 1H mace and a strong off-hand like the Furbolg Medicine Pouch.
Daggers (1H) ✓ Trainable (Darnassus/SW)
Can be learned from Weapon Master Ilyenia Moonfire in Darnassus or Woo Ping in Stormwind. High Agility daggers are usable with an Off-hand, but 2H staves/maces usually offer higher total stats.
Off-Hand Items ("Held in Off-hand") ✓ Allowed with 1H
You can equip tomes, lanterns, and relics in your off-hand only when using a 1-Handed Mace or Dagger. (e.g. Furbolg Medicine Pouch with +10 Stamina and self-heal ward).
Idols (Relic / Ranged Slot) ✓ Druid Exclusive!
Goes into your character's ranged paperdoll slot! Idols provide direct passive bonuses to shapeshift abilities: Idol of Ferocity (-3 Energy on Claw/Rake) and Idol of Brutality (-3 Rage on Maul/Swipe).
Shields ✕ PROHIBITED
Druids can NEVER equip shields! Do not buy or roll on shields. Your Bear Form (+180% to +360% Armor multiplier) naturally provides more physical damage reduction than shield-wearing warriors!
Swords & Axes (1H and 2H) ✕ PROHIBITED
Druids cannot learn or equip swords or axes of any kind. Never bid on swords, daggers are your only bladed option.
Bows, Guns, Crossbows, Wands ✕ PROHIBITED
Druids cannot shoot ranged weapons. Your ranged equipment slot is reserved exclusively for Druid Idols (Relics)!

⚔️ When Should You Use 2H vs 1H + Off-Hand?

• 95% of Leveling: ALWAYS use a Two-Handed Staff or 2H Mace!
In Classic and WoW Forever, early two-handed weapons carry massive stat combinations like +11 Strength and +4 Agility on Smite's Mighty Hammer (equivalent to +26 Attack Power and crit). Early 1-handed weapons and off-hands have split, diluted stats.

• When is 1H + Off-Hand worth considering?
Only if you obtain a top-tier Agility dagger or mace paired with a specialized utility off-hand such as the Furbolg Medicine Pouch (Timbermaw reputation, grants +10 Stamina and plants a healing totem that ticks 100 HP every 3 seconds) or high +Intellect/+Spirit off-hands when queuing as a dungeon healer!

🏛️ Alliance Weapon Master Directory (Train for 10 Silver each):

  • • Ironforge (Military Ward): Weapon Master Bixi Wobblestaff teaches Two-Handed Maces. (Visit him as soon as you reach level 18 for Smite's Mighty Hammer!)
  • • Darnassus (Warrior's Terrace): Weapon Master Ilyenia Moonfire teaches Daggers and Fist Weapons.
  • • Stormwind (Old Town): Weapon Master Woo Ping teaches One-Handed Swords (not for druids) and Daggers.
